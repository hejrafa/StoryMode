# Story Mode

A World of Warcraft addon for following the game's best quest chains from start to finish. A personal collection of storylines I played and thought were worth going back to — laid out chapter by chapter, with progress tracking and one-click quest waypoints.

Pairs well with [Dialogue UI](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/dialogue-ui) for the full experience.

---

## What it does

- **Browse campaigns** — a curated list of questlines across expansions, grouped by category
- **Chapter-by-chapter guidance** — each story is broken into chapters with context, key characters, and your next quest
- **Progress tracking** — a visual chapter track shows what you've done, what's active, and what's ahead
- **Story journal** — completed chapters are written into a journal you can revisit any time
- **One-click waypoints** — sets a TomTom arrow directly to your next quest objective
- **Smart next-quest logic** — skips completed or bypassed quests and finds where you actually are
- **Faction & class filtering** — only shows stories your character can do (class campaigns, Horde-only chains)
- **Lore chapters** — in-depth story context for major narrative moments
- **Achievement tracking** — view all associated achievements with full criteria details
- **Real-time completion banners** — see quest and chapter completion as it happens

## Usage

Open Story Mode:
```
/sm
```

Track your next quest directly from the command line:
```
/sm track
```

Or open the **World Map** and click the **Story Mode** tab on the side panel.

---

## Installation

1. Download or clone this repo
2. Place the `StoryMode` folder in:
   ```
   World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns/
   ```
3. Restart WoW or `/reload`
